Recall the most important property about the real numbers.

>[!thm] Supremum axiom.
>
>Every nonempty upper bounded set of reals has a least upper bound (called the _supremum_).

>[!note] Exercise.
>
>Suppose that $a=\sup A$. Prove that $a\in\overline A$.
